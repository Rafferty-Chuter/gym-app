# Gym AI: tester guide

Thanks for testing. This takes about five minutes to set up, then a minute or two a day.

## 1. Install
1. On your iPhone (iOS 15 or later), install **TestFlight** from the App Store.
2. Open the link we sent you on the iPhone, tap **Accept**, then **Install**.
3. In TestFlight, turn on **Automatic Updates** so fixes arrive on their own.

## 2. Set up
Open Gym AI and tap **Set up**: sex, age, height, today's weight, how long you've lifted, your goal, target weight and pace. You'll see your starting calories and macros. To change the goal later: **Progress → Change**.

## 3. Connect Apple Health (if you use it)
**Profile** (top right of Today) **→ Apple Health → Connect Apple Health**, then allow what you're happy to share. The app reads weight, body fat, sleep, steps, resting heart rate and HRV, going back 60 days the first time, and never writes to Health. To change this later: iPhone **Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Gym AI**.

## 4. Bring your training history (if you have one)
- **Hevy:** Profile → Settings → Export & Import Data → Export Data → Export Workouts. Save the file to Files.
- **Strong:** Settings → Export Strong Data. Save the file to Files. Strong files don't always say kg or lb, so first set Gym AI's **Profile → Units → Weight** to the unit you used in Strong.
- Then in Gym AI: **Profile → Import → Choose file → Preview → Import**. Importing the same file twice adds nothing.

History plus Apple Health means some of Progress fills in sooner.

## 5. Each day
- **Weight:** most mornings, after the loo and before eating. Tap **Weight** on Today (a smart scale that writes to Apple Health counts).
- **Food:** everything you eat, even roughly. **+** on Today's Nutrition card or the **Food** tab; search, or **Scan a barcode**. If you track food in MacroFactor now, log it here instead for the test.
- **Training:** **Start workout** on Today, or a routine's Start in **Train**.
- **Sleep:** comes from Apple Health when you open the app. No watch? Tap **Sleep** on Today.

There are no reminders in the iPhone app yet, so the same moment each day helps. **Progress** shows what's ready and roughly how long the rest will take: the weight trend after about a week, maintenance after about three, Connections after about 24 sessions. **See an example** shows the full screen with made-up data.

## 6. Feedback
**Profile → Send feedback** (top of Profile). Say what happened and what you expected; a screenshot helps. Anything confusing counts, and so does stopping: if you stop logging, tell us why. We'll also send three short check-ins, at weeks 2, 4 and 8.

## 7. Your data
- Your log lives on your phone. Edit or delete any entry from the day it was logged.
- Signed in (optional, **Profile → Account**)? **Profile → Account → Delete account** removes the account and everything stored in it.
- Deleting the app deletes everything on the phone.
- To ask us to delete anything else we hold, use Send feedback or the email on **Profile → Help and support**.
