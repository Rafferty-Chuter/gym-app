# Check-ins: weeks 2, 4 and 8

Three Tally forms, sent 14, 28 and 56 days after each tester's start date. Build the week 2 form, then duplicate it for weeks 4 and 8 and add their extra questions. They answer the test's four questions: do people keep logging, do they open Progress, do they believe Connections, and would they pay, how much.

**Form description (all three):** "Two minutes. Honest answers help most, including 'I stopped'."

## Every check-in

| # | Question | Tally block | Options and logic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Your email (the one you signed up with) | Email | Required. Joins the three forms up. |
| 2 | Are you still logging? | Multiple choice | Yes, most days · On and off · I've stopped |
| 3 | What got in the way? | Long answer | Show if 2 is not "Yes, most days" |
| 4 | Which is hardest to keep up? | Checkboxes | Weight · Food · Training · Sleep · None of them |
| 5 | How often do you open Progress? | Multiple choice | Most days · Once or twice a week · Once or twice so far · Not yet |
| 6 | Have you seen a Connection on Progress? | Multiple choice | Yes · Not yet · Not sure what that is |
| 7 | Which one? Roughly as it was written. | Short answer | Show if 6 is "Yes" |
| 8 | How much do you believe it? | Linear scale 1 to 5 | 1 "Don't believe it", 5 "Sure it's true". Show if 6 is "Yes" |
| 9 | Why, or why not? | Long answer | Show if 6 is "Yes" |
| 10 | What confused you, or what did you look for and not find? | Long answer | Optional |
| 11 | If it cost money today, would you pay for it? | Multiple choice | Yes · Maybe · No |

## Week 4 adds

| # | Question | Tally block | Options and logic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | How much a month would feel fair? | Number | Prefix "£" (or their currency). Show if 11 is not "No" |
| 13 | Would you rather pay monthly or yearly? | Multiple choice | Monthly · Yearly · Don't mind. Show if 11 is not "No" |
| 14 | What would make it worth paying for? | Long answer | Show if 11 is "Maybe" or "No" |

## Week 8 adds (questions 12 to 14 as well)

| # | Question | Tally block | Options and logic |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15 | Did a Connection change anything you did? What? | Long answer | Show if 6 is "Yes" |
| 16 | How would you feel if you could no longer use the app? | Multiple choice | Very disappointed · Somewhat disappointed · Not disappointed |
| 17 | Will you keep using it after the test? | Multiple choice | Yes · Maybe · No |
| 18 | Which fitness apps or devices do you pay for now? | Checkboxes | MacroFactor · Hevy Pro · Strong Pro · MyFitnessPal Premium · WHOOP · Other · None |
| 19 | Anything else? | Long answer | Optional |

Notes: pricing waits until week 4, when most people have seen some of Progress. Question 16 is the usual product-market-fit question: 40% or more "Very disappointed" is the common bar. Leave the price open (no bands) so answers aren't anchored to ranges we chose.
