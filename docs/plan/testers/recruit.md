# Recruiting testers

**Who:** 5 to 20 lifters with an iPhone who train 3 or more times a week. Ideally some already log in Hevy, Strong or MacroFactor (to test imports), a few wear an Apple Watch (sleep, HRV), and one or two aren't friends.
**When:** sign people up now; send the TestFlight link and `guide.md` only when the build is up, so everyone's 6 to 8 weeks start with a working app.
**Before posting:** check each Discord's and subreddit's self-promotion rules (some want a mod's OK first). Decide whether testers get anything after the test, such as a free year at launch; if so, add it to "What you get".

## At the gym (a message, or say it)

> I'm building an iPhone app for lifters and I'm looking for a few people to test it for 6 to 8 weeks.
>
> It puts your bodyweight trend, food, training and sleep in one place and shows how they affect each other, training above all. Things like what your maintenance really is, or whether your top sets drop after short nights. All worked out from your own logs, not generic advice.
>
> It asks you to weigh in most mornings, log your food most days and log your sessions, for 6 to 8 weeks. Hevy or Strong history comes across, and sleep comes from Apple Health if you wear a watch. Three short check-ins along the way.
>
> You get the app free, a proper read on your own numbers, and a say in what it becomes. Honest feedback is the point, including what's wrong with it.
>
> You'd need an iPhone. Interested?

## Discord or Reddit

> **Testers wanted for 6 to 8 weeks: an iPhone app that links your training, food, sleep and bodyweight**
>
> I lift and I'm building the app I wanted: one place for your weight trend, food, training and sleep over the whole of a cut or bulk, and how each affects the others. It finds the links in your own data, for example top sets a couple of kg lighter after nights under 6½ hours, or heavier after higher-carb days. Facts from your logs, not a coaching plan. Maintenance is worked out from your weigh-ins and intake, the way MacroFactor does it.
>
> **Who:** you train 3+ times a week and have an iPhone. Already logging in Hevy, Strong or MacroFactor is a plus, as is an Apple Watch.
> **What it asks:** weigh in most mornings, log food most days, log your sessions, for 6 to 8 weeks. Three short check-ins (weeks 2, 4 and 8). Hevy and Strong history imports.
> **What you get:** the app free through TestFlight, the analysis on your own data, and a direct line to me while it's being built.
> **Privacy:** your log stays on your phone unless you choose to sign in. No ads, no data sold.
>
> It's a beta, so expect rough edges. If you're in, comment or DM me with how often you train, what you log in now, and whether you wear an Apple Watch.

## Then

Keep a list (name, contact, sessions a week, current apps, watch or not). When the build is up, send each person the TestFlight link and `guide.md`, and note the date they start: the check-ins in `checkins.md` go out 2, 4 and 8 weeks after it.
